Loan Closure Policy

A loan closure request must include the loan application ID and the requester's verified identity.

Before closure, servicing staff must confirm that all required payoff amounts have been received and that no open dispute or legal hold exists.

The closure confirmation must record the closure date, the application ID, the final status, and the servicing representative or system that performed the action.

If a required verification is missing, the request must not be confirmed as closed. Escalate the request to the servicing operations team.
